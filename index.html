09 — Heritage & Brand Story

Icon Born of Conviction.

Icon was founded in 1987 by master jeweller Henri Icon, in a small atelier on the rue du Rhône, Geneva. His belief was elemental: that a truly beautiful object transcends fashion, politics, and time itself.

Today, Icon remains privately held and family-operated, guided by the same principles Henri inscribed above the atelier door: "Patience. Precision. Permanence."

Heritage Timeline

1987

Henri Icon opens the first atelier. The first collection of three rings sells within days.

1998

The first Signature Collection — twelve jewels, twelve timepieces — selling entirely through private referral.

2010

International Atelier opens in Milan and Dubai. Icon now spans three continents.

2021

Modern Heritage Collection debuts — bridging classical DNA with contemporary material innovation.

2026

A New Era of Fine Time & Jewelry — the most ambitious chapter begins today.
